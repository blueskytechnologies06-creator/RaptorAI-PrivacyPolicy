Privacy Policy for RaptorAI

Effective Date: November 9, 2026 Application Name: RaptorAI Package Name: com.raptorai Contact: blueskytechnologies06@gmail.com
Privacy Guarantee: RaptorAI is built on a 100% on-device, zero-cloud architecture. All machine learning operations run locally on your smartphone’s processor. We do not collect, transmit, share, or sell your personal prompts, images, camera captures, or voice recordings.

1. Introduction & Core Principles

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which the application RaptorAI (Application ID: com.raptorai) handles data. We are committed to complete transparency and user privacy.

Unlike traditional artificial intelligence apps that transmit your queries and files to external cloud servers, RaptorAI executes 100% of all neural inference locally on your device. No data leaves your smartphone.

2. Information We DO NOT Collect

RaptorAI does not collect, log, or transmit any personal content:

  • Chat Conversations & Prompts: Your messages, questions, and model answers remain strictly in your device memory.
  • Photos, Documents & Camera Data: Images used for OCR text extraction or vision classification are processed in local RAM and never uploaded.
  • Voice Audio & Transcripts: Audio processed by the Speech-to-Text engine (Whisper) is transcribed locally on your device hardware.
  • Personal Identifiers: We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or contacts.
  • No Advertising Identifiers: We do not collect your Android Advertising ID (GAID) or Apple IDFA for marketing purposes.
  • No Third-Party Advertisements: The app contains no advertisement networks, ad banners, or commercial trackers.

3. Crash Reporting & Diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics)

To monitor the stability of on-device neural execution across diverse Android device chips and prevent Out-Of-Memory (OOM) crashes, RaptorAI integrates Google Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase.

These diagnostic tools collect anonymous technical metrics including:

  • Crash Traces & Stack Dumps: Technical logs describing the exact line of code or ExecuTorch backend tensor error that caused a crash.
  • Device Model & OS Version: E.g., device architecture, Android OS version, and available device RAM to identify low-memory hardware constraints.
  • Feature & Screen Engagement: Anonymized screen views and tool launches to understand which on-device modalities are most actively used.

Crucially: These diagnostic logs contain zero prompt text, zero document OCR scans, and zero voice recordings. They are encrypted in transit over HTTPS and processed exclusively by Google as a service provider for app functionality and stability.

4. Network Access & Open-Source Model Downloads

Once neural models are downloaded, RaptorAI operates completely offline in Airplane Mode. The only time the application uses an internet connection is when you voluntarily choose to download open-source model weights (e.g. from verified public AI repositories such as Hugging Face).

During these voluntary model downloads:

  • Data is transferred directly between your device and the public repository over encrypted HTTPS.
  • No user telemetry, prompt history, or account details are sent.
  • Downloads can be paused, resumed, or deleted at any time through the Download Center.

5. Device Storage & Data Control

All application data—including downloaded model weights (.pte files), conversation records, and UI preferences—is stored strictly within the private app sandbox (via AsyncStorage and local storage directories).

You have complete control over your data at all times:

  • Clear Chat History: Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Clear Conversation History to instantly erase all stored messages.
  • Delete Downloaded Models: Go to Settings > Storage & Models > Delete All Downloaded Models to free up disk storage.
  • App Removal: Uninstalling RaptorAI immediately wipes all local files, cached models, and stored settings from your device.

7. Hardware Permissions

RaptorAI only requests hardware permissions when you explicitly activate a feature:

  • Microphone (Optional): Required only when recording speech in the Voice Studio for on-device Whisper transcription.
  • Camera / Photo Library (Optional): Required only when selecting an image for OCR text extraction or vision classification.
  • Internet (Network Access): Used exclusively to download public model weight files.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

RaptorAI does not knowingly collect or solicit information from children under the age of 13. Because all processing is offline and local without user accounts, no personal information is collected from any user of any age.
